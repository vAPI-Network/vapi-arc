Project description:

> vAPI reviews ERC-8183 freelance jobs on Arc before escrow funds are released.
>
> An AI evaluator can settle low-risk jobs after deterministic checks validate the job ID, deliverable hash, policy, confidence and value limit. Jobs marked HumanOnly, or escalated by the evaluator, are sent to an allowlisted Telegram auditor.
>
> The client pays 0.25 USDC for the review through Circle Gateway. The auditor receives 0.20 USDC from a Circle Developer-Controlled Wallet for submitting a reasoned verdict. The reviewer address, decision, reward, evidence hash and payout transaction are then recorded on Arc before the original escrow is completed or refunded.
